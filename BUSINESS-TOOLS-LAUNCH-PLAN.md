# Smart Digital Market — Service Business Tools Launch Plan
Updated: 2026-10-13

## Audience and opportunity
Initial broad market: U.S. owner-operated small businesses, especially local service operators (cleaning, landscaping, mobile detailing, handyman, repair). The SBA's June 30, 2025 release reported 36.2 million U.S. small businesses, but this is the *total market*, not a count of prospective buyers. Source: https://advocacy.sba.gov/2025/06/30/new-advocacy-report-shows-the-number-of-small-businesses-in-the-u-s-exceeds-36-million/

## First live free product
Job Pricing & Profit Calculator: https://cdodson21.github.io/smart-digital-market/job-pricing-profit-calculator.html

Features: editable job details, labor cost, materials, overhead, target margin, optional tax, customer-facing print view, CSV export. No account, API, or AI subscription required. No claim that this product uses AI.

## Monetization hypothesis — not yet validated
Premium **Service Business Quote & Profit Toolkit** at a test price of $19–$39.
Proposed differentiators: reusable job templates by trade, cost library, quote numbering, monthly job-profit dashboard, client follow-up tracker, branded invoice layout, and onboarding instructions. These are not built or listed yet. Sell only after testing buyer interest and producing the product.

## Validation test (7–14 days)
1. Drive visitors to the free calculator from Pinterest and relevant how-to content. Use original, non-spammy pins with a screenshot of the tool.
2. Track unique visits and outbound clicks without collecting personal customer data. Prefer privacy-conscious aggregate analytics.
3. Invite feedback about missing features via a voluntary survey or form.
4. Only create a Gumroad premium listing after there is a complete downloadable product.
5. Compare visits, clicks, wish-list/sign-up intent, and actual purchases. Don't mistake a large addressable market for proven demand.

## Operational cautions
- Tax rules differ by jurisdiction and type of service; users must verify taxable services.
- Gross profit is based only on entered costs.
- No automated AI advice is offered; do not market it as an AI product.
- Gumroad pricing as of Oct 2026: no monthly fee, direct-sale fee 10% + $0.50, and card processing may be additional; verify current official details at https://gumroad.com/pricing and https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees.
- Product must be tested on mobile and desktop before major promotion.
